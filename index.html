Agentic AI for Data Governance

Engineered a customer-facing AI agent bridging Business Impact Analysis (BIA) documents and Storage Defender data governance profiles. Built a Python-based agentic AI test library that standardizes agent development across the team and optimizes agent cost and performance. Presented technical architecture and results to IBM Storage executive leadership, including the General Manager and CTO.

Streamflow Persistence on the San Pedro River

Using a unique camera-based dataset of daily flow observations spanning over two decades, we evaluate surface water dynamics on the San Pedro River in Southern Arizona — the largest undammed river in the Southwest. We identify drivers of surface water presence and find that monsoon-season flow persistence has declined over time, governed by rising temperatures and decreasing precipitation.
